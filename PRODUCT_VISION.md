# Product Vision

## Launch Product

**Terra Policy Desk** gives frontline staff at community banks and credit unions source-linked answers from approved internal policy libraries. It helps a staff member find the governing procedure quickly, see the evidence, and know when to escalate.

## Who It Serves

- Primary user: contact-center, branch, and operations staff.
- Economic buyer: COO, Head of Operations, Head of Member Experience, or contact-center leader.
- Champion: operations or knowledge-management owner responsible for policy updates and staff consistency.

Initial customer profile: United States community banks and credit unions with roughly $250M-$5B in assets and policy-heavy service workflows.

## Job To Be Done

When a member or customer asks an operational policy question, help staff find a reliable, cited answer in seconds so they can act consistently or escalate with confidence.

## Experience Principles

- Evidence first: every claimed KB or web answer has visible sources.
- Internal by default: external research is off unless deliberately enabled.
- Honest uncertainty: no source means no grounded claim.
- Fast for repeated work: policy libraries, recent chats, and simple uploads should not require technical setup.
- Human authority remains: sensitive, unclear, or policy-changing cases need escalation.

## Paid Pilot Offer

This is a pricing hypothesis to validate with buyers:

- 30-day paid proof of value: $5,000.
- Credit the fee toward an annual subscription if the pilot converts.
- Annual target: $18,000-$36,000 for the initial staff-facing product.
- Scope: up to 25 staff, three approved policy libraries, shared evaluation questions, weekly review, and no core-banking integration.

The pilot success measures are source-cited answer rate, evaluator-rated correctness, time-to-answer, unanswered-question rate, and staff willingness to use it again.

## Positioning Boundaries

Terra Policy Desk is an internal knowledge-assist product. It is not a consumer financial advisor, an autonomous decision-maker, a replacement for compliance review, or a healthcare product at launch.

## Market Rationale

The market is large enough for a focused wedge: FDIC/FRED reported 4,278 FDIC-insured institutions in Q1 2026, while NASCUS reported 4,427 federally insured credit unions in Q4 2025. Current industry examples show staff-facing knowledge assistants in credit-union workflows, while consumer-facing financial chatbots carry higher trust and safety risk.
