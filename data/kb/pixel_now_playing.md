# Now Playing - Music Recognition

## Overview
Automatically identifies music playing around you on lock screen.

## When to Use
- Want to know song name automatically
- Identify music in public places
- Build history of songs heard

## Steps
- Go to Settings → Sound & vibration → Now Playing
- Enable Now Playing
- Song names appear on lock screen automatically
- View history of identified songs

## Notes
- Works entirely on-device (no audio sent to Google)
- Works offline
- Updates music database periodically
- Cannot identify all songs
- History saved for later viewing
