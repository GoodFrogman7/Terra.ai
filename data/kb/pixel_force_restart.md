# Force Restart Pixel

## Overview
Restart an unresponsive or frozen Pixel phone.

## When to Use
- Screen is frozen
- Phone not responding to touch
- Apps completely stuck
- Normal restart not working

## Steps
- Press and hold Power + Volume Up together
- Hold for 10-15 seconds
- Phone will vibrate and restart
- Release buttons when Google logo appears

## Notes
- Works even if screen is frozen
- Does not delete any data
- Equivalent to removing battery
- Try normal restart first: Power → Restart
