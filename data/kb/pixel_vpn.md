# VPN by Google One

## Overview
Free built-in VPN that encrypts internet traffic for privacy.

## When to Use
- Using public Wi-Fi
- Want to hide IP address
- Need encrypted browsing

## Steps
- Go to Settings → Network & internet → VPN
- Tap VPN by Google One
- Toggle Use VPN on
- VPN stays active across Wi-Fi and mobile data

## App Bypass
- Settings → VPN → Manage VPN apps
- Select apps to bypass VPN
- Useful for apps needing real location

## Notes
- Free on Pixel 7 and newer, no subscription
- No data caps or bandwidth limits
- Protects all internet traffic
- Cannot see your browsing activity
