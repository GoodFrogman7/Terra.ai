# Call Screen - Filter Unknown Calls

## Overview
Google Assistant answers unknown calls and shows real-time transcript.

## When to Use
- Receiving calls from unknown numbers
- Avoiding spam calls
- Screening calls when busy

## Steps
- When unknown call comes in, tap Screen call
- Assistant asks caller why they're calling
- Read transcript in real-time
- Choose to answer, decline, or mark as spam

## Auto-Screening Setup
- Open Phone app → Settings → Spam and Call Screen
- Enable automatic screening for:
  - Spam calls (auto-declined)
  - Possibly faked numbers
  - First-time callers
  - Private/hidden numbers

## Notes
- Available on Pixel 3 and newer
- May not work with all carriers
- Transcripts saved in call history
