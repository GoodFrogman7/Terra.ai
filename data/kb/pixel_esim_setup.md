# eSIM Setup

## Overview
Activate a digital SIM without physical SIM card.

## When to Use
- Setting up new carrier plan
- Adding second line (Dual SIM)
- Switching carriers without physical SIM

## Steps
- Go to Settings → Network & internet → SIMs
- Tap Add (+ icon)
- Scan carrier QR code, or download from carrier
- Follow activation prompts
- eSIM appears in SIM list

## Dual SIM (DSDS)
- Pixel 7+ supports two eSIMs simultaneously
- Can use physical SIM + eSIM
- Choose default for calls, texts, data

## Notes
- Confirm carrier supports eSIM for your Pixel model
- Use IMEI2 for eSIM activation
- Can have multiple eSIMs, switch between them
- Contact carrier if activation fails
