# Fix Bluetooth Issues

## Overview
Troubleshoot Bluetooth pairing and connection problems.

## When to Use
- Bluetooth device won't pair
- Audio cutting out
- Device disconnecting

## Steps
- Toggle Bluetooth off and on
- Forget device: Settings → Connected devices → Tap device → Forget
- Re-pair device
- Restart both phone and Bluetooth device
- Check Bluetooth device battery
- Move closer (within 30 feet)
- Reset network settings if nothing works

## Notes
- Remove other paired devices that might interfere
- Disable battery optimization for audio apps
- Some devices need specific pairing mode
- Check for phone software updates
