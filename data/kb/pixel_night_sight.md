# Night Sight - Low Light Photography

## Overview
Capture bright, detailed photos in dark conditions without flash.

## When to Use
- Taking photos at night
- Indoor low-light situations
- Astrophotography (stars, Milky Way)

## Steps
- Open Camera app
- Swipe to Night Sight mode (or auto-detects in low light)
- Hold phone steady for 3-6 seconds
- For astrophotography: use tripod, phone auto-detects very dark conditions

## Notes
- Works on front and rear cameras
- Keep phone as still as possible
- Tripod recommended for best results
- Astrophotography mode activates automatically when completely still in darkness
