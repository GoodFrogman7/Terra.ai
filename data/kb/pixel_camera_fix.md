# Fix Camera Issues

## Overview
Troubleshoot camera app crashes and photo quality problems.

## When to Use
- Camera app crashes
- Black screen in camera
- Photos are blurry

## Camera Crashes Fix
- Force close Camera app and reopen
- Clear cache: Settings → Apps → Camera → Storage → Clear cache
- Update Camera app via Play Store
- Restart phone
- Clear app data (resets camera settings)

## Blurry Photos Fix
- Clean camera lens with soft cloth
- Tap to focus on subject
- Hold phone steady
- Use Night Sight in low light
- Check lens isn't blocked by case

## Notes
- Black screen may be hardware issue if fixes don't work
- Ensure sufficient storage space
- Update to latest software
