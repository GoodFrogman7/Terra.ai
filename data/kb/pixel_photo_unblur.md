# Photo Unblur - Fix Blurry Photos

## Overview
AI sharpens blurry or out-of-focus photos, even old ones from other devices.

## When to Use
- Old photos from low-quality cameras
- Photos with slight motion blur
- Scanned printed photographs
- Inherited family photos

## Steps
- Open Google Photos
- Select blurry photo
- Tap Edit → Tools → Unblur
- AI processes and enhances sharpness
- Adjust intensity slider if needed
- Tap Save copy

## Notes
- Works on photos from any source
- Uses AI to reconstruct detail
- Best for slight blur, cannot fix severe blur
- Creates new copy, original preserved
