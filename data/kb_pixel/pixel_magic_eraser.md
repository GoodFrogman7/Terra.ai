# Magic Eraser - Remove Objects from Photos

## Overview
AI tool to remove unwanted people or objects from photos.

## When to Use
- Remove photobombers
- Delete distracting background objects
- Clean up cluttered photos

## Steps
- Open Google Photos
- Select photo to edit
- Tap Edit → Tools → Magic Eraser
- AI highlights suggested removals
- Tap suggestions to remove, or manually circle objects
- Tap Done to save

## Notes
- Works on any photo in Google Photos, not just Pixel photos
- Camouflage option blends objects into background instead of removing
- Best for small to medium objects
- May leave artifacts on complex backgrounds
