# Privacy Dashboard

## Overview
View which apps accessed camera, microphone, and location.

## When to Use
- Check app privacy behavior
- Find apps accessing data unexpectedly
- Audit permissions

## Steps
- Go to Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Privacy dashboard
- View 24-hour timeline of permission usage
- Tap permission type for details
- Tap app to change its permissions

## Quick Disable Camera/Mic
- Swipe down twice for Quick Settings
- Tap Camera access or Mic access to toggle off
- All apps blocked until re-enabled
- Green dot shows when camera/mic active

## Notes
- Shows camera, microphone, location access
- Identifies apps accessing data frequently
- Cannot fake or bypass privacy indicators
