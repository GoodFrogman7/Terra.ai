# Pixel Camera Specifications

## Overview
Hardware specifications for Google Pixel phone cameras.

## Pixel 9 Pro / 9 Pro XL Camera Specs
- Main: 50MP, f/1.68, OIS
- Ultrawide: 48MP, f/1.7, 123° FOV
- Telephoto: 48MP, 5x optical zoom
- Front: 42MP, f/2.2, autofocus
- 8K video recording, 4K at 60fps

## Pixel 9 Camera Specs
- Main: 50MP, f/1.68, OIS
- Ultrawide: 48MP, f/1.7
- Front: 10.5MP, f/2.2
- 4K video at 60fps

## Pixel 8 Pro Camera Specs
- Main: 50MP, f/1.68, OIS
- Ultrawide: 48MP, f/1.95
- Telephoto: 48MP, 5x optical zoom
- Front: 10.5MP, f/2.2
- 4K video at 60fps, 10-bit HDR

## Pixel 8 Camera Specs
- Main: 50MP, f/1.68, OIS
- Ultrawide: 12MP, f/2.2
- Front: 10.5MP, f/2.2
- 4K video at 60fps

## Notes
- All models feature Google Tensor chip for AI photo processing
- Night Sight, Magic Eraser, Photo Unblur on all models
- Real Tone for accurate skin tones
