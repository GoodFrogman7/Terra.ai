# Transfer Data to New Pixel

## Overview
Copy apps, photos, messages, and settings from old phone to new Pixel.

## When to Use
- Setting up new Pixel
- Switching from old Android
- Upgrading Pixel models

## Steps
- Power on new Pixel
- Follow setup wizard
- Choose Express (all data) or Customize (select items)
- Use Quick Switch Adapter or cable for fastest transfer
- Or restore from cloud backup

## What Transfers
- Apps and app data
- Photos and videos
- Contacts and messages
- Settings and wallpaper
- Wi-Fi passwords

## Notes
- Backup old phone to Google first
- Keep both phones charged during transfer
- Some app data requires re-login
- WhatsApp needs separate backup/restore
