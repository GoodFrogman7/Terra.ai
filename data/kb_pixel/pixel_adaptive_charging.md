# Adaptive Charging

## Overview
Protects battery health by learning your schedule and slowing overnight charging.

## When to Use
- Charging phone overnight
- Want to extend long-term battery health
- Regular charging schedule

## Steps
- Go to Settings → Battery → Adaptive preferences
- Enable Adaptive charging
- Charge phone overnight as usual
- Phone learns your wake time

## How It Works
- Delays full charge until you typically wake up
- Reduces time at 100% (stresses battery less)
- Only activates when plugged in before sleep
- Shows "Fully charged by [time]" on lock screen

## Notes
- Requires consistent charging schedule to learn
- Best for overnight charging
- Doesn't affect maximum charge capacity
- Protects long-term battery health
